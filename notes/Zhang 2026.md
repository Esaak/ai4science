# Zhang 2026

[источник] Yue Zhang, Xing Zheng, "POD-Kriging surrogate modeling for rapid prediction of the impact of façade protruding rib geometries on urban canyon wind flow", Building and Environment, 2026.
[источник] Аннотация: This study develops a POD-Kriging surrogate model to rapidly predict the impact of façade protruding rib geometries on urban canyon wind flow, using proper orthogonal decomposition and Kriging interpolation trained on CFD data.
[порождено] Зачем проекту: POD-Kriging суррогат для ветрового потока в городском каньоне — напрямую по теме суррогатов LES рассеяния в городской застройке.
